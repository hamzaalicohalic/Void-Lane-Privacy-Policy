Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Void Lane (the "app") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Void Lane is developed by Rana M Hamza ("we," "us"). If you have questions, contact hamzaali218822@gmail.com.

Void Lane connects to Google services — Google Play Games Services, Google Firebase Firestore, Google AdMob, and Google Play Billing — to deliver its features. Each is described below, along with a link to that service's own privacy policy.

What's stored only on your device

Your settings (control mode, sensitivity, sound) are saved locally on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

What's stored in the cloud

Your coins, best score, unlocked skins, selected skin, and upgrade levels are saved to Google Firebase Firestore and linked to your Player ID (either your Google Play Games ID if signed in, or a stable anonymous device ID if not). This allows your progress to be restored automatically if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

Your leaderboard display name and high score are stored in Firestore and are visible publicly to all players on the in-app leaderboard.

What Google's services collect

Google Firebase Firestore (cloud saves & leaderboard)

Void Lane uses Firebase Firestore to store and sync your game progress and leaderboard entry. The data stored includes your Player ID, display name, high score, coins, unlocked skins, and upgrade levels. This data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely on Google's servers under Firebase's privacy policy.

Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in)

If you choose to sign in, Play Games Services links your Google account so your progress can be synced and your high score can appear on the leaderboard. This shares your Google Play Games profile (display name, player ID) with Play Games Services under Google's own privacy policy. Signing in is optional — the game is fully playable without it, and anonymous progress is still backed up to the cloud using a stable device-based ID.

Google AdMob (ads)

Void Lane shows ads (banner, interstitial, and an optional rewarded ad for an extra life) through Google AdMob. AdMob can collect device and advertising identifiers and app interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (EEA, UK, and certain US states), the app shows a consent form before any ad request, built on Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your ad choices any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Options.

Google Play Billing (Remove Ads purchase)

The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — Google processes the transaction and tells the app whether you own the entitlement, which is then cached locally.

Permissions the app uses

Void Lane requests vibration (haptic feedback on hits and combos) and network state access (to know whether it can load an ad). It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

Children's privacy

Void Lane is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are configured to respect the consent and age settings enforced by Google Play and AdMob for the app's declared audience.

Data deletion

To remove local game data, uninstall the app. To remove your cloud-saved progress and leaderboard entry from Firebase Firestore, email us at hamzaali218822@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request – Void Lane" and include your Player ID or the Google account used to sign in. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days.

To remove data tied to your Google account (Play Games profile), manage or delete it through your Google Account permissions page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
