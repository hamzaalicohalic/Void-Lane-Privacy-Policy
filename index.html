Void Lane

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Void Lane ("the Game", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you play on Android. By playing Void Lane you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Info
  • User ID — When you sign in with Google Play Games, we store your Play Games Player ID to save your progress. If you play anonymously, we generate a stable anonymous ID from your device's Android ID (e.g. anon_a1b2c3d4). This ID never contains your name, email, or any directly identifying information.
  • Display Name — If you set a custom leaderboard name inside the game, that name is stored and shown publicly on the global leaderboard.
Game Progress
  • Coins earned and spent
  • Skins unlocked and currently selected
  • Upgrade levels purchased
  • High score
This data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore and is linked to your User ID so it can be restored on new devices.
Advertising
  • We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your consent choice shown at first launch.
  • For users in GDPR / consent-required regions, a consent dialog is shown before any ad is requested. You can update your choice in the Settings screen.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • App functionality — Syncing your coins, upgrades, skins, and score across devices.
  • Leaderboard — Displaying your display name and high score on the public global leaderboard.
  • Advertising — Showing relevant ads through Google AdMob to support free gameplay.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for any purpose other than those listed above.

3. Data Sharing

  • Google Firebase / Firestore — Your progress data is stored securely on Google's servers. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Play Games — Sign-in is handled by Google Play Games Services. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — Ad serving and Advertising ID usage. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Leaderboard display name — Your chosen display name and score are visible to all players on the global leaderboard. Do not use your real full name as your display name.

4. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS / TLS (encrypted in transit).
  • Firestore Security Rules prevent unauthorized access — only your own Player ID can write to your profile document.
  • Leaderboard scores can only ever be updated to a higher value — scores cannot be tampered with or reduced.
  • allowBackup is disabled in the app, preventing local data extraction via ADB backups.

5. Data Retention

Your game progress data is retained as long as you have the game installed or have an active cloud save. If you request deletion (see Section 6), all data associated with your Player ID will be permanently removed from our Firestore database within 30 days.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of all data we hold about you. To do so:

  • Email us at hamzaalicohalic@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request – Void Lane" and include your Player ID or the Google account used to sign in.
  • Alternatively, visit this page: hamzaalicohalic.github.io/Void-Lane-Privacy

Upon receiving your request, we will delete your leaderboard entry and cloud profile from Firestore within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Void Lane is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
